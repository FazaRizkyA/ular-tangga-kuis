# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

desktop (Windows 10/11, native Pygame window; not web). Android is a deferred extra, out of scope for this build.

## Stack

Python 3.12 + Pygame (fixed by the exam brief and the PRD). Packaged with PyInstaller (--onedir) and Inno Setup into `Setup_UlarTanggaKuis_v<VERSI>.exe` (current version 1.1, single source `config.py` VERSI). Rendering is resolution-independent: logical 1280x720 coordinates drawn at the screen's physical resolution through `kanvas.py`.

## Users

- SMK Krian 1 Sidoarjo class XI students playing in turns on one laptop (Pass & Play, 2-4 players) or one student against 1-3 bots.
- The examining teacher, who installs the app offline, plays a round, and grades it against the PBTG rubric (OOP 35, functionality 35, question bank & exceptions 15, submission & teamwork 15).

## Product Purpose

An educational snakes-and-ladders board game: every turn starts with a multiple-choice question, and only a correct answer unlocks the dice. Success = the game runs crash-free offline from the installer, demonstrates OOP clearly, and earns the group a target score of 92.

## Positioning

Knowledge gates movement: the quiz is not a side panel but the key that opens the dice each turn.

## Operating Context

- Played on school laptops, often without internet and without admin rights.
- Demoed to a teacher who reads the code: OOP must be visible (markers `# ENCAPSULATION`, `# INHERITANCE`, `# POLYMORPHISM`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 64-tile 8x8 board (confirmed 2026-11-25), zig-zag from tile 1 (bottom-left) to 64.
- Three difficulty levels chosen at setup (confirmed 2026-11-25): Mudah (6 ladders, 3 snakes, bot ~45% correct), Sedang (5, 5, ~65%), Sulit (4, 8, ~85%). Harder = more snakes and a smarter bot. Positions live in `config.py` `KESULITAN`.
- Exact finish: overshoot bounces back (confirmed).
- Correct answer +10 points and unlocks dice; ladder +5 points; wrong answer shows the key and skips the turn.
- 30-second answer timer; timeout counts as wrong (confirmed).
- Bot answers after ~1.5 s; accuracy follows the difficulty.
- Winner = first to land exactly on 64; score is informational and used for ranking.
- Questions only from `soal.json`: 300 questions, 100 per category (confirmed 2026-11-25). Matematika = aljabar dasar, eksponen, pangkat, akar, fungsi komposisi; Penjas Orkes = renang, sepak bola, bulu tangkis; Sejarah Indonesia. Plus "Campuran". Never hardcoded in .py files.
- No question repeats within a game or across Main Lagi; when a category runs out, questions come from other categories; the bank resets only after all 300 have been used.
- `try/except FileNotFoundError` and `except Exception` around loading; missing or broken file shows a message, never crashes.
- 100% offline, relative paths via `resource_path()`, bundled .ttf fonts.
- Logical canvas 1280x720, scaled to window.

## Brand Commitments

Name: "Ular Tangga Kuis". Indonesian UI copy. No other binding brand assets.

## Evidence on Hand

PRD (exported docx in user's Downloads). All art and sound are generated by `alat/buat_aset.py` or drawn in code; fonts are OFL (see `assets/SUMBER_ASET.txt`).

## Product Principles

1. The question is the turn: the eye path runs question -> answer -> dice.
2. Never crash in front of the examiner; every failure is a readable message.
3. Code is part of the product: readable, Indonesian names, OOP evident.
4. Offline and self-contained, always.
